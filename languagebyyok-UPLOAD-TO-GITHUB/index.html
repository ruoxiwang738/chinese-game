Before you begin

Academic Reading

  • The test lasts 60 minutes. The clock starts when you press Start and does not stop.
  • There are three passages and 40 questions. All questions carry one mark.
  • Closing the tab or refreshing will not give you more time - the clock runs on the server.
  • Answer every question. There is no penalty for a wrong answer, and a blank always scores zero.
  • Watch the word limits: "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS" means an answer of three words scores nothing.
  • You can flag questions to come back to, and you may change any answer until you submit.
  • When the clock reaches zero the paper is submitted automatically, exactly as in the real test.
Sit this the way you would sit the real thing: one hour, no dictionary, no breaks, phone in another room.
